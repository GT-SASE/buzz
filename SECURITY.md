# Security

This repository runs the SASE at Georgia Tech chapter site and the member
portal. The portal holds member names, email addresses, and attendance history,
so a report about it is worth sending even if you are not sure it is a real
problem.

## Reporting

Email **gt@saseconnect.org** with "Security" in the subject. Please include what
you did, what happened, and roughly when. Do not open a public issue for
anything that exposes member data or lets someone check in as another person.

We are a student chapter, not a company: expect a reply in days rather than
hours, and there is no bounty programme.

## In scope

- Anything that reads or writes another member's data
- Anything that grants officer (`ADMIN`) access other than an existing officer
  promoting someone from the roster, or `ADMIN_EMAILS` on sign-in
- Check-in fraud: awarding points to someone who was not there, or awarding them
  more than once
- Leaking a `checkInCode` to a non-officer — it is a bearer credential, and
  anyone holding one can check in

## Out of scope

- Check-in codes shared by members with each other. Rotation exists for this;
  the officer tools regenerate a code and revoke a photographed slide.
- The public site (`/`, `/about`, `/events`, …). It reads no session and no
  database, and is meant to be fully public.
- Any Google account being able to sign in and become a member. That is a
  product decision, documented in `PLAN.md`, not an oversight.
- Rate limits being per-instance rather than global. Known and documented in
  `packages/api/src/rate-limit.ts`.
- Reports generated by a scanner with no working proof of impact.

## Handling secrets

`AUTH_SECRET`, `AUTH_GOOGLE_SECRET`, and `DATABASE_URL` live only in Vercel's
environment settings and in an untracked local `.env` at the repo root. If one is
exposed, rotate it in Vercel or the Google Cloud console first, then redeploy —
rotating `AUTH_SECRET` signs everyone out, which is the intended effect.
